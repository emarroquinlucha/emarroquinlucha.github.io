The Challenge

More products. More complexity.

The app needed a clearer way to balance everyday banking with everything else Neo offered.

A few challenges stood out:

  • Everyday actions were too far away. Paying, sending, and moving money deserved a front-row spot.
  • Home had to make room. It needed to hold more accounts without becoming overwhelming.
  • New features needed a place to live. Upcoming investment products, financial tools, and support experiences called for a more flexible app structure.

The Approach

Start with what people do most.

I studied how financial apps in Canada and around the world organized their home screens, navigation, and everyday banking actions, looking for global patterns people already recognize.

The Design / 04

Exploring the future of the app.

I also explored where the app could go next, including:

  • Collapsible Account Categories Each account type folds into a single row with its total, so Home can take on new products without growing longer.
  • Universal Navigation A consistent top bar with access to Help and Security.
  • Help & Security Centres Dedicated spaces for customer support, account safety, and privacy controls.
  • Grow Tab A new home for savings, investments, and future wealth-building products.

They didn't ship, but they gave the team a direction for what comes next.

Future Concepts

The Outcome

Shipped today, with a path for tomorrow.

The refreshed Home UI, Quick Actions, and supporting product discovery entry points launched in the Neo app, so Canadians can move their money faster, starting right from Home.

The Quick Actions framework and early concepts, like collapsible account categories and a Grow tab, gave the team a clear map for where Neo goes next.

The Challenge

More products. More complexity.

As Neo introduced more products and features, the app needed a clearer way to balance everyday banking with everything else it had to offer.

A few challenges stood out:

  • Everyday actions needed to be easier to access. Common tasks like paying, sending, and moving money deserved a more prominent place.
  • More products meant more complexity. The Home experience needed to accommodate a growing number of accounts without becoming overwhelming.
  • New features needed a place to live. Upcoming investment products, financial tools, and support experiences called for a more flexible app structure.

The Design / 04

Exploring the future of the app.

To keep making room as Neo grew, I explored how Home could hold more accounts without getting longer, and a broader navigation structure to support Neo's expanding product lineup.

This included concepts for:

  • Collapsible Account Categories Each account type folds into a single row with its total, so Home can take on new products without growing longer.
  • Universal Navigation A consistent top bar with access to Help and Security.
  • Help & Security Centres Dedicated spaces for customer support, account safety, and privacy controls.
  • Grow Tab A new home for savings, investments, and future wealth-building products.

These concepts didn't ship, but they showed how the app could evolve, and gave the team a direction for fitting more products onto Home.

The Outcome

Shipped today, with a path for tomorrow.

The refreshed Home UI, Quick Actions, and supporting product discovery entry points launched in the Neo app.

Day to day, it means Canadians can move their money faster: paying, sending, and moving money now start right from Home. Behind the scenes, the Quick Actions framework and early explorations, like collapsible account categories and a Grow tab, give the team a head start on what's next.

Reflections

What I learned.

Think ahead while you can.

Working closely with our Product and Design VPs helped me realize how important it is to define, plan and document future iterations, so we're prepared for future product expansions.

Look beyond your borders.

Studying global fintechs and apps that had already solved these problems gave us a compass for what to build, and kept us on top of global patterns we could learn from.

About Me

I was born in Winnipeg, Manitoba, the coldest city on earth! I spent long winters indoors watching movies, listening to music, drawing, taking photos and, eventually, trying my hand at making websites.

I went to school for graphic design, hoping it might land me a job in the movie industry. Instead, I fell in love with making digital things move, like buttons that flip when you hover over them. I’ve been hooked on digital design ever since. (It’s also a lot easier than printing posters. Those never turn out right!)

Designing

I believe in keeping things simple and leaning on established patterns, so people can easily understand what’s in front of them. It also makes designing and building smoother, more practical and more efficient.

But every so often, we get to design something no one has seen before, and that’s still a dragon worth chasing, baby!

Thanks for visiting, and in case I don’t see ya: good afternoon, good evening, and good night.
